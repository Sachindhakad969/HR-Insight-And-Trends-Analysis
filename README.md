Key Insights: 

High Attrition in Early Career Stages: The high attrition rate in the 26-35 age group and among employees with less than 10 years of experience indicates a potential challenge with retaining younger talent.

Focus on Life Sciences Graduates: The higher attrition among employees with a Life Sciences degree might suggest a need to address specific concerns or incentives within this talent pool.

Salary Impact: The correlation between attrition and salary suggests the importance of competitive compensation to attract and retain employees.

Job Role Specificity: The higher attrition rate among Laboratory Technicians could indicate specific challenges within that role, such as workload, career development, or work-life balance.
